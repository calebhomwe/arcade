8 directions (albedo with alpha plus view-space normals) and packs the views into one atlas.

## Generated in code

- Terrain, mountains and course layout: `js/course.js`, `js/terrain.js`.
- Banners, start gate, finish arch, rails, piste poles, safety netting and kicker dye lines: `js/props.js` (canvas textures).
- Particles, carve trails and ice crystals: `js/fx.js`.
- All sound effects and the music loop are synthesised with Web Audio at runtime: `js/audio.js`.

The names "Summit Line", "Aiguille Noire" and the rival riders are fictional.
