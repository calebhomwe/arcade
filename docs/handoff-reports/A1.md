## Research (links and takeaways)
- Flappy lessons: gamedeveloper.com/design/lessons-learned-from-flappy-bird: keep it minimal, no extra mechanics. I kept one tap, added only cosmetics and goals.
- Crossy Road prize machine (crossyroad.fandom.com/wiki/Prize_Machine): coins toward unlocks drive retention; I used fixed-price skins, no chance boxes.
- Knife Hit: every 5th stage a boss, apples buy knives (apps.apple.com Knife Hit). Done as stages/bosses/checkpoints.
- Brick power-ups (Arkanoid, brick-breaker.io): expand, laser, multiball, slow, fireball. Done as capsules M/W/L/S/F/H.
- Subway Surfers missions/Word Hunt: daily seeded missions + weekly stamps.
- Color Switch (levelwinner.com guide): endless plus fixed challenge levels.
- Vampire Survivors meta (jboger.substack.com): no zero-progress runs, coins persist; upgrade about every 23 s.
- Juice it or lose it, Art of Screenshake: shake, hit-stop, squash, particles: built into fxkit.
