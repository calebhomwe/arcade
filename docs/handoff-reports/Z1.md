## Look direction (Caleb's Farm key art, not the toon demo)
Every pick uses real CC0 ambientCG textures (wood, marble, leather, stone, paper, linen, grass, rock, snow) composited under lacquer, enamel, stone, gem and metal materials with baked soft shadows, plus parchment cards, wood buttons with gold trim, dark navy glass panels, warm top light and drifting light dust. No thick ink outlines, no flat toon ramp. Honest limits: pieces in Block Blast and Minesweeper's green covered tiles are still glossy/jelly-like (closer to enamel candy than to the farm art's matte natural materials); the palettes are warm but saturated in a few looks (neon glass, ocean).
