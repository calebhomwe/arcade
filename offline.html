Your pocket arcade

Pick a game, download it once, then play from your Home Screen without a connection.

Install on your phone

iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Turn on Open as Web App if offered. Android: Chrome → ⋮ → Install and create shortcut → Install (older versions may show Add to home screen).

Open the installed icon online, download your games here, and wait for Ready offline. Then close the app, enable airplane mode and reopen it. Downloads stay on this device.
